Refund Policy

Our approach to refunds

We want every family who orders from us to feel confident in the decision. At the same time, our products are digital and delivered quickly — sometimes within hours — so whether a refund is available depends on one thing: have your forms already been delivered to you?

This page explains exactly how that works.

1. Eligibility window

Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of your purchase date. After 7 days, your purchase is considered final and no refunds are available.

2. Refunds before delivery

If you request a refund before we have delivered your forms, you are eligible for a full refund. "Delivered" means:

  • Done-For-You orders — before our team has sent your completed forms to you.
  • DIY orders — before you have downloaded the materials.

3. After your forms are delivered

Once your forms have been delivered (for Done-For-You) or downloaded (for DIY), the product is considered fulfilled and is no longer eligible for a refund. This applies even if you are still within the 7-day eligibility window described above.

4. How to request a refund

To request a refund, do one of the following:

  • Reply directly to your order confirmation email, or
  • Email info@adoptionformsexpress.com with your order number.

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.

5. Exceptions

In rare circumstances — for example, a documented technical issue on our end, or an accidental duplicate charge — we may issue a refund outside these standard terms at our discretion. If you think your situation may fall into one of these exceptions, email us and we'll take a look.
